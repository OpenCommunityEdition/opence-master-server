Discord bot

Operators apply with the bot's /apply command in the Discord server below; each application is posted to the log channel with Approve and Deny buttons, which only members of an approver role may press. The server's admins (Administrator or Manage Server) choose the channel and the roles with the bot's /configure (or /config) command. Saving connects the bot again.

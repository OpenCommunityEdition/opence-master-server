Apply to publish servers

An operator who runs OpenCE dedicated servers applies once, with the public key of their publisher key, through this master's Discord bot: applications are not taken on this website.

  1. On the server, make the publisher key with halo --server --keygen: it writes publisher.key beside config.toml and prints the public key (ed25519:...) and its key id. The private key never leaves the server.
  2. {{with .Data.Invite}}Join the master's Discord server{{else}}Join the master's Discord server (ask its contact below for an invite){{end}} and type /apply there. The bot answers you alone with a nonce to sign{{if not .Data.RequireProof}} (optional on this master){{end}}: run halo --server --sign <the nonce> on the server.
  3. Press the bot's Fill in the application button, and give a display name (shown beside your servers), the public key, what --sign printed, how many servers you run at once (and a region, if you like) and a note to the approvers.

The server can publish at once: it is told publisher_pending until an approver decides, then it is listed at its next publish. The bot tells you of the decision by a direct message. The masters share approvals, so one application is enough.
